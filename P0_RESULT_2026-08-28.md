Unpinned repeat runs on the shared execution host occasionally produced large wall-clock scheduler outliers, including >300 ms, while typical p50 remained around 80 ms for the adversarial full-verification case. Because the product SLO is wall-clock visible latency, final hard-latency acceptance is **not considered closed** by this container measurement alone. A controlled target-machine benchmark remains required.
